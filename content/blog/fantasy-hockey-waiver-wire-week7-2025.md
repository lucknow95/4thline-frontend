# Player Picks

## Anaheim Ducks (4 GP – Mon/Wed/Thu/Sat)

LW **Chris Kreider** — goal-heavy winger with trademark **SOG volume** and finishing touch. Reliable for scoring categories whenever you need early-week leverage. 

C/LW **Frank Vatrano** — brings **shots + hits** in a clean multi-cat profile. Excellent streamer for Anaheim’s four-game week; he pushes category pressure even if he doesn’t score.   

C/LW/RW **Mikael Granlund** — steady **points + shots** contributor with dependable **PP usage**. Ideal for deeper formats where flexibility and deployment matter. 

D **Olen Zellweger** — young attacker from the blue line with **shot generation + blocks**. A strong cross-category defense option for Week 7’s four-game slate. 

D **Radko Gudas** — classic banger specialist delivering **high HIT, strong BLK, and reliable PIM**. Perfect plug if you want pure peripherals during Anaheim’s four-game week.

## Boston Bruins (4 GP – Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun)

C/LW **Pavel Zacha** — excellent all-around streamer offering **points, PPP, solid shot volume, and some HIT**. Reliable in both standard and banger-heavy setups. 

D **Nikita Zadorov** — elite **HIT + PIM** upside with occasional offense. A perfect banger defenseman for a pristine four-game off-night schedule. 

---

## Buffalo Sabres (4 GP – Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun)

D **Bowen Byram** — offers dependable **shots + blocks** with occasional scoring jumps. Solid floor play during a premium four-game off-night schedule.  

LW/RW **Josh Doan** — brings **shot volume and point potential**, with a path to more minutes while Buffalo works through injuries. A flexible winger for deeper formats.  

RW **Jack Quinn** — elevated role gives him **shot volume + scoring upside**. Ideal for managers chasing goals while riding Buffalo’s perfect Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun slate.

## Carolina Hurricanes (4 GP – Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun)

D **K'Andre Miller** — provides steady **shot volume with a strong HIT/BLK floor**, and he picks up points by virtue of playing heavy minutes on a strong team. Reliable cross-category streamer.

C/RW **Logan Stankoven** — brings **point potential and a healthy shot floor**, with enough deployment to matter in deeper leagues. Strong target for clean off-night games.  

D **Sean Walker** — delivers a sturdy **shots + hits + blocks** baseline while logging strong minutes. Excellent banger-friendly defense option for a perfect Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun week.

## Edmonton Oilers (4 GP – Mon/Wed/Thu/Sat)

D **Jake Walman** — brings **point upside** with a dependable **shots + blocks** foundation. Strong streamer for managers wanting a bit of offense from the blue line. 

D **Mattias Ekholm** — steady as ever with a **reliable shot and block floor**. Usage is consistent, making him a safe play across all formats during a four-game week. 

C/RW **Jack Roslovic** — riding a **hot scoring stretch** while contributing solid **shot volume**. A strong forward add if you’re chasing offense or need a swing on a four-game slate.

## Washington Capitals (4 GP – Mon/Wed/Thu/Sat)

RW **Ryan Leonard** — supplies **solid shot volume with a dependable HIT floor**, giving him real multi-cat value even when the points aren’t flowing. Great depth add in a four-game week. 

D **Martin Fehervary** — offers a **steady baseline of shots, hits, and blocks**, making him a safe, low-event banger D. Strong floor streamer for heavy-minutes weeks like this one.
