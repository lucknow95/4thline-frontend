# Fantasy Hockey Waiver Wire Picks – Week 22 (Mar 23–Mar 29, 2026)

<br>

Welcome to **Playoff Week 2**.

At this point in the fantasy playoffs, the strategy becomes even simpler.

> **You are chasing games that actually make your lineup.**

Four-game schedules are valuable, but only when those games land on nights where your roster has space.

<br>

### Playoff Rule #2 — Startable games win matchups

In the playoffs, a **3-game player who starts all three** can easily outproduce a **4-game player who sits on the bench twice**.

This week the schedule distribution makes this especially important.

---

## The schedule reality (what actually matters)

<br>

There are **56 NHL games** in Week 22.

### Off-nights (maximum leverage)

These are the slates where streaming has the most value:

<br>

- **Mon (Mar 23): 1 game**
- **Wed (Mar 25): 2 games**
- **Fri (Mar 27): 2 games**

<br>

These nights are extremely light, meaning almost any streamer playing here will fit into fantasy lineups.

### Heavy nights (bench-clog risk)

<br>

- **Tue (Mar 24): 15 games**
- **Thu (Mar 26): 13 games**
- **Sat (Mar 28): 15 games**

<br>

Sunday sits in the middle:

<br>

- **Sun (Mar 29): 6 games**

<br>

The real leverage once again comes from **Monday, Wednesday, and Friday**.

## The only thing we’re chasing: startable volume

### Tier 0 — The week-winning schedule edge

<br>

#### **New York Rangers (NYR)** — **4 games**, elite off-night distribution

**NYR schedule**

<br>

- Mon vs OTT  
- Wed @ TOR  
- Fri vs CHI  
- Sun vs FLA  

<br>

### Rangers Player Targets

<br>

**Alexis Lafrenière (LW/RW)**  
Lafrenière is averaging **just shy of 0.7 points per game** this season with **2 shots per night** and **over a hit per game**. He is also seeing an elevated role since the Panarin trade, including time on the **top power play**, giving him strong upside this week.

<br>

**Will Cuylle (LW/RW)**  
Cuylle is a **must-own this week in banger leagues**, averaging **just under 0.5 points per game** with **almost 2 shots**, **just shy of 3.5 hits**, and **almost a block per night**.

<br>

**Vladislav Gavrikov (D)**  
Gavrikov is averaging **over 0.4 points per game** with **almost a shot and a half** and **1.3 blocks per game**, making him a solid blue-line option for managers needing category support.

<br>

**Braden Schneider (D)**  
Schneider continues to provide strong peripheral value, averaging **over a shot**, **just over 2 hits**, and **over 1.5 blocks per game**.
