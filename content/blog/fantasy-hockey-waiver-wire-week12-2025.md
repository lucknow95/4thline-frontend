# Fantasy Hockey Waiver Wire Picks – Week 12 (Dec 22–28, 2025)

Week 12 is the **Christmas-week schedule** — and it plays completely different than a normal fantasy week.

---

## The schedule reality (what actually matters)

There are **only FOUR NHL slate days** this week:

- **Mon (Dec 22):** light slate (**8 games**)  
- **Tue (Dec 23):** **heavy** (**26 games**)  
- **Wed–Fri (Dec 24–26):** **no games** (league-wide Christmas break)  
- **Sat (Dec 27):** **heavy** (**26 games**)  
- **Sun (Dec 28):** light-ish (**10 games**)

Because of the break, most teams play **1–2 games**. That means:

- **3 games = best (priority stream targets)**  
- **2 games = usable (fallback options only)**  
- **1 game = worst (avoid streaming unless it’s a star)**  

This week is not about off-nights — it’s about **pure man-games**.

---

## Quick Jumpstart (pre-break attack: Mon–Tue)

Your biggest edge comes from **banking games before the break**.

### Best pre-break two-game burst (Mon + Tue)

- **Seattle Kraken (SEA)** — **Mon/Tue/Sun**  
- **Philadelphia Flyers (PHI)** — **Mon/Tue/Sun**  
- **Los Angeles Kings (LAK)** — **Mon/Tue/Sat**  

If you add a streamer from **SEA / PHI / LAK**, you can realistically get **two games in two nights** before rosters clog up.

---

### Monday openers (Mon + weekend)

Monday is only **8 games**, making it one of the easiest nights to stream into.

- **Tampa Bay Lightning (TBL)** — **Mon/Sat/Sun**  
- **Columbus Blue Jackets (CBJ)** — **Mon/Sun**  
- **Anaheim Ducks (ANA)** — **Mon/Sat**  
- **St. Louis Blues (STL)** — **Mon/Sat**  
- **Vancouver Canucks (VAN)** — **Mon/Sat**  

---

## The only thing we’re chasing: 3-game teams

There are **8 teams** with **3 games** this week. These should be the **foundation** of your streaming plan.

### Tier 1 — Best schedule shapes (3 GP with Mon and/or Sun leverage)

These teams give you flexibility on the light slates.

- **Seattle Kraken (SEA)** — **Mon/Tue/Sun**  
- **Philadelphia Flyers (PHI)** — **Mon/Tue/Sun**  
- **Tampa Bay Lightning (TBL)** — **Mon/Sat/Sun**  
- **Los Angeles Kings (LAK)** — **Mon/Tue/Sat**  

---

### Tier 2 — Still strong (3 GP, heavier-night lean)

Still excellent volume this week, just slightly harder to fit.

- **Toronto Maple Leafs (TOR)** — **Tue/Sat/Sun**  
- **Chicago Blackhawks (CHI)** — **Tue/Sat/Sun**  
- **Detroit Red Wings (DET)** — **Tue/Sat/Sun**  
- **New York Islanders (NYI)** — **Tue/Sat/Sun**  

---

## Best 2-game fallback schedules

If the 3-game teams are gone, prioritize **Mon or Sun exposure**.

- **Columbus Blue Jackets (CBJ)** — **Mon/Sun**  
- **Pittsburgh Penguins (PIT)** — **Tue/Sun**  
- **Montreal Canadiens (MTL)** — **Sat/Sun**  

Avoid teams that are **Tue/Sat only** unless your roster is unusually open.

---

## One-game warning (do not stream)

Only two teams play **one game** this week:

- **Utah Mammoth (UTM)** — **Tue only**  
- **Winnipeg Jets (WPG)** — **Sat only**  

In a week where your opponent can stream **three games**, one-game streamers are a clear disadvantage.

---

## How to build your Week 12 stream plan

A simple framework:

1. **Start with the 3-game teams**  
   - SEA / PHI / LAK / TBL are your cleanest builds.

2. **Bank games early when possible**  
   - Mon–Tue combos give you a massive edge before the break.

3. **Use Sunday as your catch-up lever**  
   - Only 10 games → easy lineup access.

4. **Ignore 1-game teams completely**  
   - Even good players can’t overcome the volume gap.

## Player Picks

### Seattle Kraken (3 GP – Mon/Tue/Sun)

RW **Jordan Eberle** — Brings **good shot volume** with a real chance to chip in points. A solid add if you want top-six scoring upside without sacrificing SOG.

LW/RW **Eeli Tolvanen** — Provides a strong multi-cat profile: **steady shots**, **extra blocks for a winger**, and **solid hits**, with legitimate point upside. The key bonus this week: he’s currently running on **PP1**, which raises his ceiling on a short slate.

D **Adam Larsson** — A classic banger-league defense streamer. He gives you a dependable floor of **shots and hits**, plus **decent blocks**, and the schedule (Mon/Tue/Sun) makes it easier to actually get all three games into your lineup.

---

### Philadelphia Flyers (3 GP – Mon/Tue/Sun)

LW/RW **Owen Tippett** — Has been producing **decent points** while maintaining a useful blend of **shots and hits**. He’s one of the better all-format streamers on Philly because he doesn’t need a goal to be valuable.

D **Cam York** — A clean “quiet points” add from the blue line. He’s been providing **assists** with a workable **block floor**, making him a nice fit if you’re chasing helpers without punting peripherals.

D **Nick Seeler** — Pure category glue. Seeler gives you a stable baseline of **shots**, with **solid hits and blocks** layered in. A strong choice if you need reliable peripherals across three games.

RW **Garnet Hathaway** — If your matchup is about brute force stats, Hathaway is the specialist. He’s **elite for hits and PIM**, and in a week with fewer games league-wide, those categories can swing quickly.

---

### Los Angeles Kings (3 GP – Mon/Tue/Sat)

D **Drew Doughty** — Back from injury and trending toward relevance fast. He offers **shot volume**, an **okay block floor**, and the real appeal is the chance for **power-play points** as his minutes stabilize.

C **Anze Kopitar** — Always carries a path to offense. If you need a points swing from a center slot, Kopitar is a steady, low-drama option with a dependable role.

D **Brandt Clarke** — Ownership is rising for a reason: he’s been putting up **useful points** with a respectable floor in **shots and blocks**. One of the better upside defense adds on a short week.

---

### Tampa Bay Lightning (3 GP – Mon/Sat/Sun)

C **Anthony Cirelli** — Offers **decent shot volume** and strong opportunity to contribute points within Tampa’s scoring environment. A safe add when you want offense without overthinking it.

C/LW **Nick Paul** — A well-rounded streamer with **decent shots and hits** plus real scoring upside. The Mon/Sat/Sun layout also gives you a nice chance to use him as a weekend catch-up piece.

---

## Closing Notes (Quick Hits You Can Action)

- Treat **3-game teams as the new 4-game teams** this week.  
- **SEA / PHI** offer the best overall schedule shape.  
- **TBL** is the top weekend-swing option.  
- **Sunday matters** — build for it.  
- Avoid **UTM / WPG** streamers entirely.
