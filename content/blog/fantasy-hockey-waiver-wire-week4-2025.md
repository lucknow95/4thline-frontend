# Fantasy Hockey Waiver Wire Picks – Week 4 (2025)

Week 4 (Oct 27–Nov 2) flips the script: **off-nights are rare**.  
The slate again leans into **Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday**, which means your best edges come from any team that sneaks in **Monday, Wednesday,Friday, or Sunday**.

<br><br>

It’s a tighter week for streamers, but smart swaps can still net **3–4 extra man-games** if you hunt the light windows.

---

## How We Pick Waiver Adds

Same process, adapted to a scarcer off-night board:

<br><br>

1. Start with **4-game teams**.  
2. Prioritize any schedule touching **Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun** (even one off-night matters this week).  
3. Use the [Rankings page](/rankings) with **GTW + per-game toggle** to find starters you’ll actually fit.  
4. Late-week, open the [Optimizer](/optimizer) to roll a **Sunday stash → Monday** (Week 5) pivot.

<br><br>

Heavy slates (Tue/Thu/Sat) clog benches. **One clean off-night start can be the difference** in tight H2H matchups.

## Why This Works

It’s still **man-games math**:

- A skater at **3 SOG + 2 HIT per game** yields **12 SOG + 8 HIT** in a 4-game week.  
- **Off-night starts** keep that production in your lineup instead of on the bench.  
- Chain your week: **grab Mon/Wed/Fri**, then pivot into a **Sunday stash → Monday (Week 5)**.
