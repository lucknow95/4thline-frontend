# Fantasy Hockey Waiver Wire Picks – Week 21 (Mar 16–Mar 22, 2026)

Welcome to **Playoff Week 1**.

Now the only thing that matters is this:

> **Can your waiver add actually get into your lineup?**

This is not the week to save moves, hold fringe depth, or chase a “nice schedule” that turns into bench waste.

### Playoff Rule #1 — Stop saving moves

If your playoffs start this week, **unused adds are wasted adds**.

There is no prize for being clever next week if you lose this matchup first.

Use your adds. Chase starts. Win the schedule.

---

## The schedule reality (what actually matters)

There are **56 NHL games** in Week 21.

### Off-nights (maximum leverage)

These are the slates where streaming has the most value:

- **Mon (Mar 16): 5 games**
- **Wed (Mar 18): 6 games**
- **Fri (Mar 20): 5 games**

These are the nights where lineup flexibility actually exists.

### Heavy nights (bench-clog risk)

These are the slates where your “4-game streamer” can still rot on the bench:

- **Tue (Mar 17): 9 games**
- **Thu (Mar 19): 11 games**
- **Sat (Mar 21): 11 games**
- **Sun (Mar 22): 9 games**

Sunday is still usable in some formats, but this week the true leverage comes from **Monday, Wednesday, and Friday**.

#### **Calgary (CGY)** — **4 games**, with **3 on off-nights**

**CGY schedule**

- Mon @ DET  
- Wed vs STL  
- Fri vs FLA  
- Sun vs TBL  

### Calgary Flames Player Targets

The Flames are not a strong team overall, but they do have several players who can provide category help.

**Blake Coleman (C/LW/RW)**  
Coleman is averaging **almost 0.5 points per game**, with **just shy of 2.5 shots**, **2.5 hits**, and around **0.6 PIM per night**. He can also chip in the occasional **shorthanded point**.

**Mikael Backlund (C)**  
Backlund is producing **over 0.5 points per game** with **just shy of 2.5 shots per night**.

**Adam Klapka (RW)**  
Klapka is a **banger league specialist**, averaging **almost 4 hits per game** with **over 1.5 PIM**.
