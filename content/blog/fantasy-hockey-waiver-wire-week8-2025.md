# Player Picks

## Dallas Stars (4 GP – Tue/Wed/Fri/Sun)

C/RW **Tyler Seguin** — steady **shot floor** with the ability to rack up points when he gets rolling. A veteran scorer who benefits from Dallas’ rare Tuesday game and clean Sunday finish.  

D **Lian Bichsel** — heavy **HIT volume**, making him a strong banger-league target for a four-game slate. Particularly valuable because he rarely sits on heavy nights. 

D **Esa Lindell** — dependable **block producer** with consistent minutes. Quiet category glue piece who fits perfectly into a four-game week.

C/LW **Jamie Benn** — back from injury and offering a mix of **shots + scoring potential**. Provides sneaky value if you need offense with some physicality. 

---

## Ottawa Senators (4 GP – Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun)

C **Shane Pinto** — scoring consistently this season with strong **shot volume**. One of the top mid-tier center streamers for Week 8.   

C **Dylan Cozens** — offers **shots + hits + scoring threat** every night. Classic multi-cat center who thrives in four-game weeks.   

LW/RW **Claude Giroux** — reliable **shot floor** and steady point production. A safe streaming option who plays meaningful minutes in all situations.  

D **Artem Zub** — bringing a dependable mix of **shots, hits, and blocks** this season. Excellent floor defenseman to round out your weekly lineup.

---

## Washington Capitals (4 GP – Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun)

C/LW **Connor McMichael** — strong **shot floor** with proven scoring touch from last season. Great depth play in all formats. 

RW **Ryan Leonard** — offers **shots + hits**, with occasional scoring punch. Ideal for managers needing multi-cat support.  

D **Martin Fehervary** — sturdy **shot/hit/block** baseline with consistent ice time. Classic banger-friendly streamer. 

D **Rasmus Sandin** — provides a blend of **shots + blocks** with power-play capability. Good all-around defensive streamer. 

D **Matt Roy** — steady **hits + blocks** contributor with low-risk minutes. Great late-week pickup for peripheral boosts.
