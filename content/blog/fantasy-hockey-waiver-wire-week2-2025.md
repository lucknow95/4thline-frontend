## Why This Works

Streaming is all about **man-games math**:  

- A skater averaging **3 shots and 2 hits** gives you **12 shots and 8 hits** over 4 games.  
- You can’t score from your bench — **off-nights convert dead bench slots into production**.  
- Chaining schedules (early-week ➜ mid-week ➜ late-week ➜ next-week stash) compounds your advantage.
