# Fantasy Hockey Waiver Wire Picks – Week 19 (Mar 2–Mar 8, 2026)

Week 19 is back to a standard Monday–Sunday matchup — and this is exactly where schedule leverage separates good managers from great ones.

There’s no Olympic break chaos this time. Just clean streaming opportunity.

This week is about:
- **Attacking off-nights**
- **Avoiding heavy-slate bench waste**
- **Turning one add into two startable games**

If you're battling for playoff positioning, small edges matter again.

---

## The schedule reality (what actually matters)

There are **58 NHL games** this week.

### Off-nights (maximum leverage)
- **Mon (Mar 2): 6 games**
- **Wed (Mar 4): 5 games** (best slate of the week)
- **Fri (Mar 6): 7 games**
- **Sun (Mar 8): 7 games**

### Heavy nights (bench-clog risk)
- **Tue (Mar 3): 12 games**
- **Sat (Mar 7): 12 games**

### Medium
- **Thu (Mar 5): 9 games**

**Translation:**  
The edge lives on **Mon / Wed / Fri / Sun**.  
The trap is streaming players who only play **Tue/Sat**.

## Player Pickups

### Detroit Red Wings (DET)

RW **Patrick Kane** — Kane remains a strong offense streamer when you need points and power-play upside. He’s averaging **over 0.7 points per game** with **nearly 2.5 shots per night**, and remains a consistent man-advantage threat.

D **Simon Edvinsson** *(IR — monitor)* — If he’s nearing a return, Edvinsson is a strong multi-cat defense add. He’s averaging **around 0.3 points per game** with **over a shot**, **over a hit**, and **over two blocks per night**.

D **Ben Chiarot** — A reliable banger-league option. Chiarot is averaging **nearly one PIM per game** with **over two hits** and **over two blocks per night**.

---

### Anaheim Ducks (ANA)

RW **Bennett Sennecke** — Sennecke continues to provide offense with real shot volume. He’s averaging **over 0.75 points per game** with **well over two shots per night**.

C/LW/RW **Mikael Granlund** — Elite positional flexibility with steady production. He’s averaging **just over 0.7 points per game** with **over two shots per night**.

D **Radko Gudas** — One of the best pure banger adds available. Gudas is averaging **nearly 3.5 hits** with **almost two blocks per game**.

LW **Chris Kreider** — A scoring streamer with shot upside. He’s averaging **around 0.6 points per game** with **just shy of two shots per night**.
