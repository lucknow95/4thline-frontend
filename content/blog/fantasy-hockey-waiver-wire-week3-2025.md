## Why This Works

It’s **man-games math**:

- A skater at **3 SOG + 2 HIT per game** yields **12 SOG + 8 HIT** in a 4-game week.  
- **Off-night starts** keep that production active instead of sitting on your bench.  
- Chain your schedule: **early-week ➜ mid-week ➜ late-week ➜ next-week stash**.
