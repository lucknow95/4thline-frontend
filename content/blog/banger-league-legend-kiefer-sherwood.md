## Role & Usage
- **Line/Deployment:** Expected to rotate in Vancouver’s middle six (3RW or 2RW when injuries hit).  
- **Special Teams:** Spot PK usage; little to no PP time.  
- **Team Fit:** Vancouver’s north–south pace and forecheck pressure amplify his physical game perfectly.  

Sherwood doesn’t need big minutes — his impact is felt every shift. Vancouver’s coaching staff loves energy players, and he can maintain fantasy relevance with 12–14 minutes a night if he keeps delivering 5–6 hits per game.

---

## Fantasy Outlook (This Season)
- **Best for:** Banger or multi-cat leagues emphasizing HIT, SOG, and PIM.  
- **Streaming Value:** High. Whenever the Canucks have 4-game or off-night-heavy weeks, Sherwood becomes a must-add.  
- **Risks:** Limited offense ceiling; must hold ice-time around 13–14 min/g to stay fantasy-relevant.  

If his Vancouver usage includes a second-unit PK role, expect even more hit opportunities, and maybe a cheeky SHP.
