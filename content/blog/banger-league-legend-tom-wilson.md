## Role & Usage
- **Line/Deployment:** Top-six RW; can ride shotgun with top playmakers or drive a heavy second line.  
- **Special Teams:** **PP1** net-front/bumper, playing with elite talent; **PK** Often PK2, lending an opportunity for SHP.  
- **Team Fit:** Washington still leans on Wilson’s physical identity; his game lives below the dots and along the wall.

---

## Fantasy Outlook (This Season)
- **Best for:** Multi-cat/banger formats (HIT/PIM/SOG + G/A).  
- **Roster Value:** Set-and-forget in bangers; A stream in points-only if PP time dips.  
- **Risks:** Suspensions/injury history; offense can be streaky if PP deployment changes.

With steady PP looks and top-six minutes, a **20–30 goal, 200+ hit, 80–100 PIM** season is firmly in range.
