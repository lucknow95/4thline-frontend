# Fantasy Hockey Waiver Wire Picks – Week 5 (2025)

Week 5 runs **Mon, Nov 3 → Sun, Nov 9**. It’s a classic “front-load off-nights, pivot to weekend churn” setup.  
**Chicago** uniquely hits **Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun**—a clean off-night quad that minimizes bench conflicts. **Vancouver, Seattle, Toronto** also offer **three off-nights** each, and there’s a large **Sat/Sun back-to-back cluster** (great for goalie streams and last-day pushes).

---

## How We Pick Waiver Adds

1. Start with **4-game teams**.  
2. Prioritize schedules touching **Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun** (off-nights).  
3. Use the Rankings page (**GTW + per-game toggle**) to confirm starters you’ll actually fit.  
4. Late-week, churn toward **Sat/Sun B2B** skaters/goalies to squeeze 1–2 extra starts.

## Why This Works

It’s still **man-games math**:

- A skater at **3 SOG + 2 HIT/GP** yields **12 SOG + 8 HIT** in a 4-game week.  
- **Off-night starts** keep that production in your lineup (not on the bench).  
- Chain your week: **grab Mon/Wed/Fri**, then pivot into the **Sat/Sun B2B** pool.
